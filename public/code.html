Manual Pairing

No problem — you can complete the pairing manually:

  1. Open WhatsApp on your phone.
  2. On Android: tap the ⋮ three dots in the top-right, then tap Linked devices.
  3. On iPhone: tap Settings in the bottom-right, then tap Linked devices.
  4. Tap Link a device, then tap Link with phone number instead.
  5. Enter the code shown above on your phone to complete pairing.

Include your country code. Do not use +, spaces or dashes.
